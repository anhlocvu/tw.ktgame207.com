You are so bored with your current nickname in the game, you want to increase the security of your account.
We will help you do these things.
## change name:
I will give you specific steps to change your name:
1. press f8 to open option.
2. press enter at account settings.
3. press enter to open change name.
4. in the input box: please type new name you want to change.
5. after type new name, you can tab to find Change Name Button and press enter to confirm.
6. after, you will see a line of text: Your account name has been successfully changed. press enter to skip.
7. After that, you will temporarily leave the game and you will now log in with your new name.
## change password:
Here's how to protect your account from unreliable dangers:
1. press f8 to open option.
2. press enter at account settings.
3. press enter to open change password.
4. in the input box: please type new password you want to change.
5. after type new password, you can tab to find Change Password Button and press enter to confirm.
6. after, you will see a line of text: the password has been changed to success. press enter to skip.
7. After that, you will temporarily leave the game and you will now log in with your new password.
