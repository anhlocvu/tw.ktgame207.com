## cooking food with microwave:
These are extremely simple steps for you to get the best quality dishes:
1. Go find the nearest microwave.
2. At the microwave, press alt+space to open the door.
3. Open your bag and hold the food you need to cook in your hand.
4. Press space to put food in the oven.
5. Press alt+space to close door.
6. Press space to start cooking.
7. After cooking is complete, press alt+space again to open the door.
8. Press space to take cooked food from the microwave.
9. Finally, press alt+space to close.
